Before the camera turns on

Poker Face reads your face.

  • What it uses. Your webcam, analysed by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK running on this computer, to estimate facial expression and a pulse reading.
  • What it keeps. Nothing. No video, image or reading is saved to disk. Closing the app ends the session.
  • What leaves this app. The stream overlay receives game state only (timer, points, a rounded pulse number) over a local address. The SDK makes its own authenticated requests to Presage's servers, which can be linked to the API key's account. Its optional telemetry is switched off. Presage's privacy policy covers what the SDK itself sends.
  • What it is. A game. The pulse reading is for entertainment and is not a medical measurement.
  • Who is on camera. Only you. Do not point it at anyone who has not agreed.

The camera stays off. You can agree whenever you are ready.
